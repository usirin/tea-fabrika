# 0499: No tile rule trades subtle, accessible or easy on the eyes for another, and when a quiet color cannot carry a fact a word or a shape is added, never more color

**What this decides:** the bar every tile rule must clear, in today's version. `next/` reads it as
a lesson.

## Context

Quiet tiles and accessible tiles were argued as a trade. From
[tile guidelines](../docs/direction/tile-guidelines.md#the-bar).

## Decision

**Subtle, accessible and easy on the eyes are one bar.**

- Subtle: a tile does not compete with its contents. A mark with no job goes.
- Accessible: WCAG 2.2 AA holds by default, with no setting needed. A preference only goes above
  that floor.
- Easy on the eyes: no full-chroma lines, no large bright areas, no motion on a tile, no pure white.
- When a quiet color cannot carry a fact, add a word or a shape, never more color.

## Consequences

- Every tile part below carries a non-color cue: a word, a letter, a weight or a dash.
