# 0142: The aimed look stays the only look, with no weekly open look at a program's full shot grid

**What this decides:** UI verification adds no recurring open look; the owner looks only where
[0476](0476-the-owners-look-is-aimed.md) aims the look.

## Context

[UI verification](../docs/direction/ui-verification.md#the-smaller-calls) left one call open with
no lean, C6: should the owner also take one open look a week at the full shot grid of one program,
as Linear does with its Quality Wednesdays? 0476 already settled the aimed look: the shots no law
explains, every new screen, and the critical tasks. The owner decided C6 on 2026-11-11.

## Decision

**The aimed look of 0476 stays the only look, and C6's weekly open look is not added.**

- A weekly look is a recurring chore. AGENTS.md says the protection goes in code, not in a chore
  (Changing the product). A bug class an open look keeps finding becomes a law, not a habit.
- 0476 is unchanged: aimed looks, each ending in a `fix` or `noise` rating per finding.

## Consequences

- A bug that only an open look finds is caught later, by use. The note records one such find: the
  focused title bar that looked the same as the resting one was found by an open look at round 2's
  shots, before any law named it. Under this decision, a bug like that waits until someone meets it
  in daily use.
- The note's doubt on U7 still stands: an aimed look misses what nobody listed.
- Not decided here: an agent taking an open look on a schedule.
