tsh$ programs
say  Says a text once, and ends. tsh's echo.
seq  Counts from 1 up to a number, one value at a time, and ends.
double  Doubles each number it is told, and ends when its input closes.
upper  Says each text it is told in capital letters, and ends when its input closes.
sleep  Waits, then ends. Stopped before then, it ends stopped.
true  Ends at once, and well: exit status 0.
false  Ends at once, and fails: exit status 1.
tally  Keeps a running total of the numbers it is told, and says it after each one. A number that would carry the total past the safe whole numbers is refused, and the total stays. The total is kept: started again under the same run id, it goes on from it.
counter  Counts presses of +1 until done, and says the count. It has a window.
ticket  One ticket, read from the ledger: its title, whose turn it is, its body, its facts, and its properties. Answers its open ask, comments, asks for changes, closes and reopens. It has a window.
map  The map of today's programs: their inputs, outputs and wires, and where one ticket stands on the plan's nine steps. It has a window.
trace  One ticket's runs laid out in time: a track per step, a clip per run, the waits on a person, the answers, and what each step cost. A playhead replays the facts up to a time. It has a window.
tracker  Hands out every open ticket, then listens: a ticket a stage sends back is handed out again.
triage  Sorts a ticket: yes goes on to plan, no is skipped.
plan  Plans a ticket and asks its owner to approve the plan; an approved plan goes on to build.
build  Builds a ticket's plan into a commit; a built ticket goes on to review.
review  Reviews a built ticket; red checks send it back to build, a pass goes on to ship.
ship  Ships a reviewed ticket: pushes it, opens its pull request and merges it.
